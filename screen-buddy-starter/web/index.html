What it is

Think of a sticker that dances. Not a video in a box: just the person, with nothing around them, standing on top of your work.

  • Floats over everything On Windows and Android a buddy stays on top of your apps. You choose its size and how see-through it is.
  • Moves when you do It can stay still until you touch it, dance steadily, dance while you type, or dance when there is sound.
  • Real people, real moves Each buddy is cut out from a short video of a creator who agreed to be in the app.
